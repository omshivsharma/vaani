Namaste 👋

Vaani calls your contacts, speaks your message, listens to their answer and notes what they said. It can also send the same invitation on WhatsApp and SMS.

How a call works

  1. Vaani dials the number from your own phone through Windows Phone Link (free, uses your SIM).
  2. When they pick up, it plays your opening line, in your recorded voice if you recorded one.
  3. It listens, understands with Gemini (free tier), and replies in a natural Indian voice.
  4. When it's done, it saves the outcome (coming / not coming / call back…) and a short note.

Settings

1. Gemini key (free)

This is Vaani's brain. Get a free key at aistudio.google.com/apikey → Create API key, and paste it here. It's saved only in vaani/.env on this laptop.

2. Phone bridge (for real calls)

  1. Open Phone Link on this laptop, link your Android phone, and turn on Calls (it pairs over Bluetooth).
  2. Install VB-Audio Virtual Cable (free, from vb-audio.com/Cable), then restart the laptop.
  3. Windows Sound settings → Input → choose CABLE Output while Vaani is calling. (Switch back to your normal mic afterwards.)
  4. Make a test call to your own second number from Call & send, and check that you hear Vaani on the other phone.
